How to tag a touch
Pause at the touch. Type the jersey number, press the touch key, then the result key (and a digit for the detail), then Enter. Tab switches team. For an attack or serve, click the court where the ball landed. A point-ending result closes the rally; otherwise press Point when the rally ends. Z undoes the last touch. You can tag every touch or only some: only what you tag is counted.
